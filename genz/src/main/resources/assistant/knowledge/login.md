# Login and signing in

To login (log in or sign in) to NextGoal, open the sign-in screen, enter your registered phone number and password, and submit the login form. Use the same phone number you registered with.

If login fails, check the phone number and password and ensure the device has a working connection to the backend. If you forgot your password, use the password-reset flow and follow the OTP verification steps. Never share your password, OTP, or PIN with the assistant.

If you do not have an account, complete registration and phone verification first. Changing an account role does not change the login password.
