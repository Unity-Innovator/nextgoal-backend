# Account security and password reset
If you forgot your password, use Forgot Password, enter your phone number, and verify the password-reset OTP before setting a new password. A registration OTP and a password-reset OTP belong to different verification flows.

Keep your password, PIN, OTP, and session tokens private. The assistant does not need these secrets. Sign in through the app's login screen. The assistant cannot recover or reveal passwords or PINs.
