# Deposits and withdrawals
Use the Deposit screen to choose a savings goal and enter an amount. For withdrawals, use the Withdraw screen and choose the source goal. Review the details in the app before confirming any transaction.

The assistant only explains navigation. It cannot deposit, withdraw, transfer money, or confirm whether a transaction succeeded. Check the transaction history in the app for recorded activity. This guide does not specify fees, limits, processing times, or eligibility; do not assume those details.
