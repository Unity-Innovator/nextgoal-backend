# Savings goals
Open Goals to view your savings goals. The All, In Progress, Completed, and On Hold filters select which goals are displayed. Tap a goal to view its details. Use the plus button to open the create-goal screen.

Creating a goal involves naming it, setting a target amount, and choosing a target date. The assistant can explain these steps but cannot create or change goals, read your current balance, or confirm transactions. Use the app screens to manage your goals.
